LOCKOUT

Privacy and terms, in plain words. Last changed 20 November 2026.

Who runs it

Lockout is made and run by Gub Games, in the United Kingdom. Questions, or to have your data deleted: lockoutgame@outlook.com.

What we keep

  • Your email address and password. Used to log you in and to send you a confirm or a password reset email. We never see your password: it is stored scrambled (hashed) by our login service.
  • Android tester sign-ups. Only if you choose BE AN ANDROID TESTER in Options: the Google account email you type, used only to invite you to the Google Play test. Ask us and we'll remove it.
  • Your progress. Your level, cosmetics, decks and settings, so you can carry on from another phone or computer.
  • Your player name and rank on the Top 500. If you reach Grandmaster (even as a guest), your player name, points and avatar go on the world board. These are public: anyone playing can see them.
  • Friends. Your friend code, your friends list and friend requests, and whether you're online and what you're doing (in the menus, in a match), so your friends can see it and invite you. Your friends also see your avatar, Battle Pass tier and rank. You can set yourself to Busy or Offline in the friends list.
  • Your room. How you've laid out My Room, with your player name, avatar and pet, so other players can visit it.
  • Online matches. While you play online (Quick Play, Ranked or with friends), the moves of that match pass through our server. Each player's view of the cards is sealed so only they can read it. Chat messages in a room pass through the same way. Nothing is kept after the room closes.
  • Reports. If someone reports a player for their chat, we keep that player's last few chat lines with the report so we can check it.
  • Shared links. If you opened the game from a link with a tag on it (for example one we posted on social media), we count which tag it was. Nothing else about you goes with it.
  • Your device. The game saves some things on your phone or in your browser (settings, Remember me, and all of a guest's progress). Deleting the app or clearing your browser's data removes them.

We don't sell your data and we don't track you round the web. There are no adverts in Lockout at the moment; if we add them, we'll update this page first and ask for your permission where the law says we must.

Nothing in Lockout costs real money yet. If we start selling things, payments will go through Apple, Google or a payment service, and we never see your card details.

Who else handles it

Logins, saves and online rooms are run by Supabase, on servers in London. Emails are sent by our email service. The game itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any website, sees your IP address when you load it. The game also loads its font from Google Fonts.

How long, and deleting it

We keep your account until you delete it. To delete it: log in, then press DELETE ACCOUNT on the first screen. Your login, progress and Top 500 rows go straight away and can't be brought back. You can also email us and we'll do it for you. Under UK law you can also ask us for a copy of what we hold about you.

Children

Lockout is meant for players aged 13 and over. If you are under 13, please don't make an account.

The rules of playing

  • Be fair. Don't cheat, use bots, or break the game on purpose, and don't pick a player name or send chat that is rude, hateful or pretends to be someone else. We can remove names, reset ranks or close accounts that do.
  • Gold, cards and cosmetics in the game are part of the game. They have no money value and can't be sold or swapped for real money.
  • We're always changing the game. Things can be added, taken out or rebalanced, and we may wipe the Top 500 at the end of a season.
  • Lockout is given as it is. We try hard to keep it running and your progress safe, but we can't promise it will never break or lose something.
  • Lockout, its art, music and words belong to its maker.

Help and support: contact us, delete your account, report a player, questions.
