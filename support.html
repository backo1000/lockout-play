LOCKOUT

Help and support. Last changed 20 November 2026.

Get in touch

Lockout is made by Gub Games, in the United Kingdom. Something broken, a question, or an idea? Email lockoutgame@outlook.com. A real person reads every one, usually within a day or two.

If something went wrong, it helps to tell us your player name, which phone or browser you play on, and what you were doing when it happened.

Delete your account

Log in, then press DELETE ACCOUNT on the first screen. Your login, your progress and your Top 500 rows go straight away and can't be brought back.

Can't log in any more? Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll delete it for you.

Playing as a guest? A guest has no account: everything is kept on your phone or in your browser, so deleting the app or clearing your browser's data removes it.

Report a player

In an online match, tap their name in the chat and choose REPORT. That mutes them for you and sends us their last few chat lines so we can check them. You can also just mute someone without reporting them.

For anything else (a rude player name, cheating, someone pretending to be someone they're not), email us with their player name and what happened. We look at every report and can rename players, reset ranks or close accounts.

In the phone apps, chat starts with the bad language filter on. You can switch it on or off in Options.

Questions

Guest or account?

A guest plays straight away, with no email, and can play the bots and practise. Everything a guest earns is kept only on that phone or browser. Make an account (an email and a password) to keep your progress safe, carry it to another phone or computer, play online and add friends. Your cards, gold and cosmetics come with you.

What is the Ranked beta season?

Ranked is open now so you can practise climbing. On launch day everyone goes back to the bottom rank and the Top 500 starts empty, so the race is fair for everyone. You keep the rank titles and gold you win during the beta.

What are the beta rewards?

Everyone who tested Lockout before launch gets two things to say thank you: the TESTED ON HUMANS card back and the SIR BETA THE FIRST avatar. Nobody can get them after the beta. They arrive the next time you log in with the account you tested on. If yours didn't arrive, email us.

I lost my progress

If you played with an account, log in with the same email and it will come back. If you played as a guest, progress lives on that phone or browser only, so we can't bring it back if the app was deleted or the browser data was cleared.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in board, choose FORGOT PASSWORD and we'll email you a link to set a new one. Check your junk folder if it doesn't turn up.

Does anything cost money?

Not yet. Nothing in Lockout costs real money at the moment. Gold, cards and cosmetics are part of the game and have no money value.
